## 📖 About this Course

**Advanced Learning Algorithms** is the second course of Andrew Ng's **Machine Learning Specialization**, taught by the co-founder of Coursera and DeepLearning.AI, professor at Stanford.

## 📚 Course Content

### Neural Networks
- Building intuition for neural networks
- Implementation with TensorFlow and Keras
- Forward propagation in Python and NumPy (from scratch)
- Activation functions (ReLU, Sigmoid, Linear)
- Multiclass classification with Softmax

### Training a Machine Learning Model
- Choosing the cost function and optimizer (Adam)
- Bias/variance diagnosis
- Learning curves
- Regularization and model selection
- Iterative ML development process

### Decision Trees
- Building decision trees
- Impurity measures (entropy)
- Regression trees
- Tree ensembles: Random Forest, XGBoost
- Trees vs. neural networks comparison

## 🎯 Skills Acquired

- Build and train a neural network with TensorFlow
- Diagnose and fix a model's bias/variance
- Apply ML development best practices (train/dev/test, error analysis)
- Build decision trees and tree ensembles
- Choose between neural networks and trees depending on context

## 🏆 Impact on My Journey

This course strengthened my understanding of neural networks and gave me a rigorous methodology for diagnosing and improving a ML model — well beyond simple implementation.
