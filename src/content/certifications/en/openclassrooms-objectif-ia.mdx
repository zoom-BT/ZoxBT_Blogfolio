## 📖 About this Course

**Objectif IA : initiez-vous à l'intelligence artificielle** is an introductory course by **OpenClassrooms**, tracing the history of AI and laying out the field's conceptual foundations.

## 📚 Course Content

- History and evolution of artificial intelligence
- Different types of AI (narrow, strong, general)
- Introduction to Machine Learning and Deep Learning
- Fields of application of AI
- Ethical and societal issues

## 🎯 Skills Acquired

- Understand the major milestones in the history of AI
- Distinguish between different types of artificial intelligence
- Identify the main fields of application
- Grasp the ethical issues of AI

## 🏆 Impact on My Journey

This course laid the very first theoretical foundations of my journey in artificial intelligence, before going deeper with more technical courses (Machine Learning, Deep Learning).
