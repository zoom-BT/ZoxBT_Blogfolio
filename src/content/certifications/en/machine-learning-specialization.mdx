## 🏆 Full Specialization

The **Machine Learning Specialization** brings together the 3 courses taught by **Andrew Ng** (co-founder of Coursera and DeepLearning.AI, professor at Stanford):

1. ✅ **Supervised Machine Learning: Regression and Classification**
2. ✅ **Advanced Learning Algorithms**
3. ✅ **Unsupervised Learning, Recommenders, Reinforcement Learning**

## 📖 Overview

This specialization is the reference for building solid foundations in modern Machine Learning: from linear regression to neural networks, from decision trees to reinforcement learning.

## 🎯 Skills Covered

- Linear and logistic regression, gradient descent, regularization
- Neural networks and TensorFlow
- Decision trees and tree ensembles
- Clustering, anomaly detection
- Recommender systems
- Reinforcement learning and Deep Q-Learning

## 🏆 Impact on My Journey

Completing this specialization marks a key milestone: a complete theoretical and practical foundation in Machine Learning, from fundamentals to advanced topics, applied throughout my AI projects.
