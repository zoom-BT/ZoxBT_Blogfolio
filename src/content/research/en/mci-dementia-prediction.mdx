## Context

Eight-week research internship (24 July to 24 September 2026) at **UMMISCO**, principal supervision **Pr Norbert Tsopze**, with **Bernard Fongang, PhD** (AI-BOND, UT Health San Antonio) and **Pr Paulin Melatagia Yonta**.

The topic is multimodal prediction of conversion from mild cognitive impairment (MCI) to dementia in **NACC**, **ADNI**, and **Framingham**. Individual-level data access (DUA) was not obtained during the internship, so there was no analysis on these cohorts and no conversion result.

Currently awaiting validation of the analysis plan and data access to move forward.

## A pre-specified protocol, not yet executed

A literature review shows that conversion models are mostly built on ADNI and rarely externally validated (Xia et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024). The analysis plan describes a future longitudinal cross-cohort study of 2- and 5-year conversion risk. These choices were fixed before data access:

- One row per participant at the index visit
- Incomplete profiles kept
- Late fusion
- Pre-fixed external validation rotations
- Calibration, decision-curve analysis, and fairness
- Identify the smallest useful data package

Execution starts with a feasibility audit. The protocol has not been run.

## Cross-sectional pilot on public OASIS-2

Completed pilot on public OASIS-2: 150 participants at the baseline visit, with CDR excluded from the predictors. Six models separate a composite positive group (64 demented + 14 future converters) from 72 non-demented.

Test AUCs were 0.67–0.75. The random forest reached 0.75 (bootstrap 0.58–0.89).

This AUC does not measure 2- or 5-year conversion. The 4 future converters in the test set were all missed: the model recognizes present dementia, not future conversion. The test set was used for model selection, so the result is exploratory.

## Lumenscreen

Teaching demonstrator **Lumenscreen** (FastAPI + Next.js), with a layered design and an input contract that rejects CDR and identifiers. It is not a medical device.
