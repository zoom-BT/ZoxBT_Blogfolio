## Context

This two-month research internship is part of my final-year engineering program (ING-5) at ENSPY, in collaboration with **Pr Melatagia**.

## Subject

**Multimodal prediction of MCI-to-dementia conversion**, across three reference cohorts in neuroscience research:

- **NACC** (National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center)
- **ADNI** (Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative)
- **Framingham** (Framingham Heart Study)

The goal is to build a model able to predict, from multimodal data (clinical, cognitive, possibly imaging), whether a patient with Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) will progress to dementia.

## Approach

The work combines:

- Harmonizing heterogeneous data across three distinct cohorts
- Multimodal modeling (tabular, longitudinal, possibly imaging data)
- Evaluating cross-cohort generalization

## Status

This project is **ongoing**. Results and a more detailed methodology description will be added as the internship progresses.
