## Context

This pre-engineering research internship is part of the first semester of my final year (ING-5) at ENSPY, supervised by **Pr Tsopze Norbert** (University of Yaoundé I), in collaboration with **Pr Bernard Fongang** (University of Texas, USA). The second-semester engineering internship, required for the degree, will follow separately.

## Subject

Building **leakage-resistant models** for predicting the risk of conversion from Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) to dementia, over a **2 to 5 year horizon**, across three reference cohorts in neuroscience research:

- **NACC** (National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center)
- **ADNI** (Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative)
- **Framingham** (Framingham Heart Study)

The data combines clinical information, imaging, and biomarkers.

## Approach

The work focuses on three areas:

- **External validation**: assessing how well models generalize across cohorts
- **Calibration**: ensuring predicted risk scores reflect real-world probabilities
- **Interpretable baselines**: reference models (Cox regression, XGBoost) analyzed with SHAP to understand the identified risk factors

## Status

This project is **ongoing**. Results and a more detailed methodology description will be added as the internship progresses.
